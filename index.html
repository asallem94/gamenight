About Game Night

Game Night makes games for phones and tablets. Monster Mania 360 is a Game Night game, and it is covered by the same privacy policy and content rights statement as the studio.

Monster Mania 360

Monster Mania 360 is a first-person monster hunt. You move through a stage, aim, and clear waves of monsters. You can play a campaign on your own or join a co-op hunt with other players.

Progress, settings, and in-game items stay on your device. Online play uses a hunter name you choose so other players in that match can see who you are. Optional purchases add gems and energy. The game also shows ads, including ads you can watch for a reward.

On the App Store the app is published as Monstermania, with bundle identifier com.gamenight.monstermania.
