Privacy Policy

This policy covers Double Agent, a multiplayer game published by Game Night, on the platforms where the app is offered.

Game Night is the developer. Contact gamenightmp@gmail.com with privacy questions.

This website only publishes this information. It does not use accounts, cookies, or analytics.

What the game collects

Information you provide

Double Agent asks for a display name of at least three characters. That name is sent to the game’s Firebase service so the other players in the same game can see it. You do not need an account, email address, phone number, or your real name to play.

Information stored on your device

The app saves a random identifier on your device and uses it to recognize that player when you return. Deleting the app deletes it.

Multiplayer

Creating or joining a game sends your display name and the moves needed to run the match, such as cards placed, attacks, and votes, to Firebase. That information is used to operate the game. Game Night does not use it to build a marketing profile.

Purchases

Double Agent does not offer in-app purchases.

Advertising

Double Agent shows banner ads through Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may receive and use information such as your device’s advertising identifier, IP address, device type, and interactions with ads in order to provide, measure, and limit ads.

You can limit this in your device settings: on iOS, Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; on Android, the ads or privacy settings for your device.

Google’s policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

What we do not collect

  • Precise location
  • Contacts, photos, camera, or microphone audio
  • Health or fitness data
  • A Game Night account
  • Payment card details

How we use information

  • To recognize the player on this device
  • To run a multiplayer game and show the name you chose
  • To show and measure ads through Google

Game Night does not sell your personal information. We share information only with the services above, and only for those purposes: Apple or Google for distribution, Google for advertising, and Firebase to run the game.

How long we keep it

The identifier stored on your device remains until you delete the app. Multiplayer match data is used to run the game and is not kept as a marketing profile.

Your choices

  • Choose a name you are comfortable showing other players.
  • Limit ad tracking in the device settings.
  • Delete the app to remove the identifier stored on the device.

To ask about a name used in a game, email gamenightmp@gmail.com.

Children

Double Agent is not directed at children under 13. Game Night does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided a display name or other information, email us and we will delete it from the multiplayer service where it is still held.

International use

The game may be used in more than one country. Information may be processed in the United States and in other countries where Apple, Google, and Firebase operate.

Changes

Game Night may update this policy. The date at the top of this page will change when it does. The new policy applies to Double Agent.
