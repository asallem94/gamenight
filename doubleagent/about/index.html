Double Agent

Double Agent is a multiplayer game from Game Night. Game Night connects people through multiplayer games, and Double Agent is one of those games.

You play on a colored team and try to occupy every base. Some players are double agents, and their true team is another color.

On your turn you draw division cards, place them on your team’s bases, and attack an adjacent base when one is ready. Both sides reveal their cards, and the stronger color takes the base. If the attack fails, your teammates vote on whether you are a double agent. Play continues until one team holds the map.

You enter a name, then create a game or join one with a code. The name is what the other players in that game see. On the App Store and Google Play the app is Double Agent, with identifier com.gamenight.doubleagent.
