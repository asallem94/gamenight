Privacy Policy

This policy covers Monster Mania 360, a game published by Game Night, on iOS and Android.

Game Night is the developer. Contact gamenightmp@gmail.com with privacy questions.

This website only publishes this information. It does not use accounts, cookies, or analytics.

What the game collects

Information you provide

Multiplayer uses a hunter name. That name is saved on your device and sent to the game server so players in the same match can see it. You do not need an account, email address, phone number, or your real name to play.

Information stored on your device

The app saves game data locally on the device, including:

  • Settings
  • Campaign progress and player stats
  • In-game gems, energy, scrolls, and upgrades
  • Daily reward state
  • Your hunter name and last multiplayer server
  • A record of purchases the app has already granted

This information stays on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Multiplayer

When you play online, the game sends your hunter name and the actions needed to run the match, such as movement and shots, to Game Night’s game server. That information is used to operate the hunt. Match state is kept for the session. Game Night does not use it to build a marketing profile.

Purchases

Optional purchases, such as gems, energy, and a starter pack, are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. Game Night does not receive your card number or full payment details. The store, and a purchase-verification service, confirm the purchase so the app can grant the items. Those items are stored on your device.

Advertising

Monster Mania 360 shows ads through Google AdMob, including banner ads and optional rewarded ads. Google and its advertising partners may receive and use information such as your device’s advertising identifier, IP address, device type, and interactions with ads in order to provide, measure, and limit ads.

You can limit this in your device settings: on iOS, Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; on Android, the ads or privacy settings for your device. You can also decline a rewarded ad.

Google’s policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

Motion controls

If you aim with motion controls, the app reads the device motion sensors to look around. That sensor data is used on the device for controls. Game Night does not collect it as a profile.

What we do not collect

  • Precise location
  • Contacts, photos, camera, or microphone audio
  • Health or fitness data
  • A Game Night account

How we use information

  • To run the game, save progress, and remember settings
  • To run a multiplayer match and show hunter names
  • To grant items from a purchase
  • To show and measure ads through Google

Game Night does not sell your personal information. We share information only with the services above, and only for those purposes: Apple or Google for purchases and distribution, Google for advertising, a purchase-verification service to confirm receipts, and the host of the multiplayer server to run a match.

How long we keep it

Information stored on your device remains until you delete the app. Multiplayer match data is kept for the session and is not kept as a lasting player profile. Purchase records held by Apple or Google follow that store’s policy.

Your choices

  • Choose a hunter name you are comfortable showing other players, and change it later.
  • Skip online play. Campaign progress still stays on the device.
  • Limit ad tracking in the device settings, and skip rewarded ads.
  • Skip purchases. The game does not require them.
  • Delete the app to remove the information stored on the device.

To ask about a hunter name used in multiplayer, email gamenightmp@gmail.com.

Children

Monster Mania 360 is not directed at children under 13. Game Night does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided a hunter name or other information, email us and we will delete it from the multiplayer service where it is still held.

International use

The game may be used in more than one country. Information may be processed in the United States and in other countries where Apple, Google, and our hosting and verification providers operate.

Changes

Game Night may update this policy. The date at the top of this page will change when it does. The new policy applies to Monster Mania 360.
