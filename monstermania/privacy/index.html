Privacy Policy

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By downloading, installing, accessing, or otherwise using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, do not download, install, access, or use the App.

1. Introduction and scope

This Privacy Policy (this “Policy”) describes the practices of Game Night Inc. (“Game Night,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) with respect to information collected in connection with the mobile application known as Monster Mania 360 (the “App”), as offered on iOS and Android. This Policy explains the types of information we may collect, and our practices for collecting, using, disclosing, retaining, and protecting that information.

This Policy applies whenever you download, install, access, or otherwise use the App. It does not apply to information we collect offline, to other Game Night applications, or to information collected by any third party under that third party’s own policies. Other Game Night applications are governed by their own privacy policies.

The website on which this Policy is published is provided solely to make this Policy and related information available to the public. That website does not require an account and does not use cookies or analytics.

If you do not want Game Night to collect, use, or disclose information in the manner described in this Policy, do not download or use the App. By downloading, installing, accessing, or otherwise using the App, you agree to this Policy. We may change this Policy from time to time, as described in “Changes to this Policy” below. Your continued use of the App after a change is posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised Policy.

2. Information we collect and how we collect it

We collect information from and about users of the App directly from you when you provide it, and automatically when you use the App, as described below.

2.1 Information you provide

Multiplayer play uses a hunter name chosen by you. That name is stored on your device and is transmitted to the game server so that other players participating in the same match may view it. You are not required to create an account, and you are not required to provide an email address, telephone number, or your legal name, in order to use the App. You may change your hunter name within the App.

If you contact us, we collect the information you choose to include in that correspondence, which may include your email address and the contents of your message, together with our records of that correspondence.

2.2 Information stored on your device

The App stores game data locally on your device, including the following:

  • settings;
  • campaign progress and player statistics;
  • in-game gems, energy, scrolls, and upgrades;
  • daily-reward state;
  • your hunter name and the last multiplayer server you used; and
  • a record of purchases that the App has already granted, so that those items are not granted again.

That information remains on your device. Deletion of the App from the device deletes it. We do not use locally stored game data to construct a marketing profile of you.

2.3 Multiplayer gameplay

When you play online, the App transmits to Game Night’s game server your hunter name and the gameplay actions reasonably necessary to operate the match, including movement and shots. We use that information to operate the hunt. Match state is retained for the session. We do not use it to construct a marketing profile of you.

2.4 Purchases

The App offers optional purchases, including gems, energy, and a starter pack. Those purchases are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. Game Night does not receive your payment card number or your full payment details. The applicable store, together with a purchase-verification service acting on our behalf, confirms the transaction so that the App may grant the corresponding items. Those items are stored on your device. Purchases are not required in order to use the App.

2.5 Advertising

The App displays advertisements through Google AdMob, including banner advertisements and optional rewarded advertisements. Google LLC and its advertising partners may receive and use information associated with your device, which may include your device advertising identifier (including Apple’s Identifier for Advertisers or Google’s advertising identifier), Internet Protocol address, device type, and information about your interactions with advertisements, for the purpose of providing, measuring, limiting, and improving advertisements. Those parties may use tracking technologies, including device identifiers, in connection with that advertising. We do not control those third parties’ technologies or the manner in which they use information they collect. Their practices are governed by their own policies. Google’s privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy.

A rewarded advertisement is optional. You may decline it. Declining a rewarded advertisement does not prevent banner advertisements from being displayed.

2.6 Motion controls

If you elect to aim using motion controls, the App reads motion sensors on your device in order to direct the view within the game. That sensor data is processed on the device for the purpose of controls. We do not collect it as a profile, and we do not transmit it to Game Night for that purpose.

2.7 Information we do not collect

Through the App, we do not collect:

  • precise geolocation;
  • contacts, photographs, camera images, or microphone audio;
  • health or fitness information; or
  • a Game Night account.

3. How we use the information we collect

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • to operate the App, including to save progress, remember settings, and grant items associated with a confirmed purchase;
  • to conduct a multiplayer match and to display hunter names to players in that match;
  • to provide, measure, and limit advertisements delivered through Google, including optional rewarded advertisements;
  • to respond to your inquiries and to provide support in connection with the App;
  • to protect the rights, property, and safety of Game Night, users of the App, and the public, and to detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, or other unlawful activity, including unauthorized purchases;
  • to comply with applicable law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request, and to enforce our agreements; and
  • to maintain, secure, and improve the operation of the App.

We do not use gameplay information to build a profile of you for marketing by Game Night, and we do not sell your personal information.

4. How we may share your information

We may disclose aggregated or de-identified information that does not reasonably identify you or your device for any lawful purpose.

We may also disclose information about you or your device in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers. We disclose information to vendors and service providers who process it on our behalf in order to operate the App, including the host of the multiplayer server, which receives your hunter name and the gameplay actions necessary to conduct a match, and a purchase-verification service, which receives information reasonably necessary to confirm a store receipt so that the App may grant the corresponding items.
  • Advertising partners. Google and its advertising partners receive advertising-related information as described in Section 2.5, in connection with advertisements displayed in the App.
  • Distribution and payment platforms. Apple Inc. and Google process purchases and may process information in connection with distribution of the App through the Apple App Store or Google Play, subject to their own terms and privacy policies. Records of purchases held by Apple or Google are governed by the applicable store’s policy.
  • Legal and safety. We may access, preserve, and disclose information if we believe in good faith that doing so is reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, regulation, legal process, or a lawful request by a public authority, including to meet national security or law enforcement requirements; to enforce our rights; to protect the rights, property, or safety of Game Night, our users, or others; or where we believe the App is being used in connection with unlawful activity, fraud, or a threat to the health or safety of any person.
  • Business transfers. We may disclose information in connection with, or during negotiation of, any merger, sale of assets, financing, reorganization, bankruptcy, or acquisition of all or a portion of our business. If such a transaction occurs, the recipient will be required to handle the information in a manner consistent with this Policy.
  • With your direction. We may disclose information where you consent or direct us to do so.

We do not share your personal information with third parties for their own direct-marketing purposes.

5. Your choices, including advertising

You may exercise the following choices:

  • select a hunter name that you are willing to have shown to other players, and change that name later;
  • decline to play online; campaign progress remains stored on the device;
  • limit the use of your device advertising identifier for personalized advertising, as described below, and decline any rewarded advertisement;
  • decline to make purchases; purchases are not required in order to use the App; and
  • delete the App, which removes the information stored on the device.

To request information about a hunter name used in multiplayer play, or to submit any other privacy request, email gamenightmp@gmail.com.

Advertising helps to support the cost of providing the App. You may not be able to prevent advertisements from being presented while you use the App. You may, however, limit personalized advertising through the settings of your device. On Apple devices, those controls are available under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, as further described by Apple at support.apple.com/en-us/102420. On Android devices, those controls are available in the advertising or privacy settings for the device, where you may reset your advertising identifier or opt out of personalized advertisements. Game Night does not control those device-level mechanisms and is not responsible for their continued availability, accuracy, or effectiveness. Use of a device-level control may limit personalized advertising from third parties generally, and not solely advertising delivered in the App.

You may also be able to opt out of certain interest-based advertising through programs administered by third parties, including the Network Advertising Initiative and the Digital Advertising Alliance. Game Night does not control those opt-out mechanisms.

The website on which this Policy is published does not recognize or respond to browser-initiated “Do Not Track” signals. The App does not use browser tracking of that kind.
